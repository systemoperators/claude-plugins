# Sources

Every source the skill's rules rest on. Each was read on 2026-11-20. The rules are ours, in our own words: copyrighted texts are paraphrased and linked, never copied. "Detail" names the research file in onlyfonts/docs/RESEARCH/TYPOGRAPHY-SKILL that holds the page numbers, quotes and notes.

## Books and guides

- S1 Butterick's Practical Typography, Matthew Butterick. https://practicaltypography.com (key rules, point size, line length, line spacing, all caps, bold or italic, mixing fonts, headings, system fonts). Copyrighted, paraphrased and linked. Detail: CANON.md, PAIRING.md.
- S2 The Elements of Typographic Style, 4th ed. 2012, Robert Bringhurst, read through Richard Rutter's http://webtypography.net and excerpts at https://sarahsaroufim.github.io/binding/elements.html. Book all rights reserved; Rutter's text CC BY-NC 4.0, ideas only. Detail: CANON.md.
- S3 Thinking with Type, Ellen Lupton, companion pages archived at https://web.archive.org/web/20180414065005/http://www.thinkingwithtype.com/contents/letter/ and the Text chapter. All rights reserved, paraphrased and linked; the live domain has lapsed. Detail: CANON.md.
- S4 Stop Stealing Sheep and Find Out How Type Works, 4th ed., Erik Spiekermann and Ferdinand Ulrich. https://static.googleusercontent.com/media/fonts.google.com/en/us/knowledge/stop_stealing_sheep.pdf. Copyrighted (2022), paraphrased and linked. Detail: CANON.md.
- S5 Refactoring UI, Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger. https://refactoringui.com/previews/line-height-is-proportional, other rules through a reader's notes at https://rjseymour66.github.io/webdev/refactoring-ui/ (secondary). Paid book, paraphrased and linked. Detail: CANON.md.
- S6 Combining Typefaces, Tim Brown, Typekit 2013. https://blog.typekit.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/combiningtypefaces.pdf. Copyrighted, paraphrased and linked. Detail: CANON.md.
- S7 More Meaningful Typography, Tim Brown, A List Apart 2011. https://alistapart.com/article/more-meaningful-typography/. Copyrighted, paraphrased and linked. Detail: CANON.md.
- S8 Type Scale calculator. https://typescale.com/. Ratio names and values only (facts). Detail: CANON.md.
- S9 Material Design 3 type scale, Google. https://github.com/material-components/material-components-android/blob/master/docs/theming/Typography.md and androidx TypeScaleTokens.kt. Apache 2.0. Detail: CANON.md.
