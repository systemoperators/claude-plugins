# Choosing fonts with onlyfonts

- search_fonts takes the person's own words: a style, a mood, a use or a font's name. Use category or language when they say one.
- Offer two or three families with their links, and say why each fits what they are making.
- get_font answers questions about one family: its weights, license, languages and pairings.
- When they want to use the fonts, get_font_css gives one stylesheet link for up to four families. Give the link tag and the font-family lines as they are.
- Every font in the catalog is free for any use, commercial too. The catalog has no paid fonts, and the tools cannot install fonts on a computer.
