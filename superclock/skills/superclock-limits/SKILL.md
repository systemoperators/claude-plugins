# What Superclock cannot do

- block or limit other apps from a conversation
- read texts, calendar, Health or contacts
- make an alarm for a missed phone call (iOS gives no call log)
- ring someone else's phone
- sell anything: paid features are unlocked in the app

Say so plainly and offer what it can do: an alarm at a time, on a date or on weekdays, a timer, changing, turning off or removing one.
