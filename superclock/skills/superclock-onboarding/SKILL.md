# Superclock onboarding

Call list_alarms. The first call connects Superclock: the person signs in on the Superclock page and allows it. Then say which alarms are there, or that there are none yet.

When a tool answers that no iPhone is signed in, alarms have nowhere to ring yet. Tell them, in three steps:

1. get Superclock from the App Store
2. on the iPhone: Settings, Account, Sign in with Apple, with the same Apple ID they used here
3. tell you when it is done, and ask again
